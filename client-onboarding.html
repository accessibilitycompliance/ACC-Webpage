Accessibility Compliance Canada Inc. · Version: 2026.1.0 · Jurisdiction: Ontario, Canada

Agreement

1. The Compliance Ecosystem (Scope of Services)

ACC grants Client a non-exclusive, revocable license to access the AODA Compliance Portal, mapping to the 11 provincial filing questions, including:

  • Triple-Pillar Training: Automated modules for Customer Service, IASR, and Ontario Human Rights Code.
  • Documentation Engine: Templates for Written Policies, Multi-Year Accessibility Plans (MYAP), and Feedback Capture protocols.
  • AI Widget: Web overlay for 90+ language support and automated WCAG 2.1 AA digital remediation.
  • The Audit-Ready Binder: A centralized, time-stamped registry of all compliance actions and training logs.

2. AI Widget Performance & Limitations

  • 2.1 Technical Remediation: The AI Widget provides automated, “best-efforts” digital remediation. Client acknowledges that the Widget cannot modify physical architecture, hardware, or legacy/third-party software outside the Widget’s direct script control.
  • 2.2 Quality & Aesthetics: ACC does not guarantee that the automated fixes will meet the Client’s subjective aesthetic preferences or align with all third-party proprietary accessibility scanning tools.
  • 2.3 Functionality: The Widget is a supportive tool; it does not replace the requirement for the Client to maintain an inherently accessible underlying web architecture.

3. Templates & “Grey Spot” SOPs

  • 3.1 License to Use: All templates and SOPs are “Baselines” licensed to the Client for internal use only.
  • 3.2 Client Responsibility: Client is solely responsible for tailoring these “Baselines” to their specific physical premises, employee headcounts, and operational realities.

4. Fees, Subscriptions, & Recurring Renewal

  • 4.1 Flexible Billing: Services are provided on a subscription basis (Monthly or Yearly) at the rate selected by the Client at the time of signup.
  • 4.2 Automatic Renewal: All subscriptions automatically renew for successive periods of the same duration unless cancelled via settings prior to the renewal date.
  • 4.3 Non-Refundable: Due to the immediate digital delivery of proprietary templates and training logs, all fees are non-refundable.

5. Data Sovereignty, Privacy & Security

  • 5.1 Canadian Residency: ACC warrants that 100% of Client “Evidence Data” is hosted on Canadian-based servers (AWS Canada Central).
  • 5.2 Security Standards: ACC utilizes SOC 2 Type 2 compliant infrastructure. Data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS).

6. Strategic Liability Protections

  • 6.1 Final Attestation: Client acknowledges that the Director or Officer of the Client company is the sole legal signatory for the AODA report. ACC is an “Evidence Provider,” not a legal representative or the “Accountable Signatory.”
  • 6.2 Limitation of Liability: ACC’s total cumulative liability for any claim — including government fines, audit failures, or data loss — is strictly capped at the total fees paid by the Client in the 3 months preceding the claim.
  • 6.3 No Class Action: Client agrees that any dispute resolution will be conducted on an individual basis only and not in a class or representative action.
  • 6.4 Audit Support Disclaimer: ACC provides data for an audit; however, ACC does not represent the Client in front of any regulatory body. ACC is not liable for the findings of a Ministry audit or a third-party accessibility auditor.
  • 6.5 Specialist Referrals: ACC is not liable for the work, advice, or conduct of any third-party specialists (Legal, Engineering, HR) referred through the Portal.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada.
